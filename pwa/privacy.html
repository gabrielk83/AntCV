Privacy notice and AI use

AntCV · version 2026-10-23

Document purpose. What AntCV does with your data, which AI it uses, who receives your data and how you use your rights (GDPR Art. 13, EU AI Act Art. 50).

1. Who is responsible

The operator of AntCV is the data controller for your account, your stored profile and your saved applications.

Contact: [operator name and contact e-mail - to be filled in before public launch].

2. What AntCV is, and what it is not

  • AntCV drafts your own CV and cover letters with generative AI (large language models). You review the text and you are its author.
  • AI text can be wrong, invent facts or carry bias. Check every line before you send it.
  • AntCV is not a recruitment, screening or candidate-ranking tool. It does not decide anything about you and gives no score to an employer. No automated decision with legal or similar effect (GDPR Art. 22) is made.
  • Exported files are marked as AI-assisted: a short notice in the footer and a machine-readable tag in the file properties (EU AI Act Art. 50(2)).

Sensitive data. A CV does not need health, religion, political views, union membership, ethnic origin or sexual orientation (GDPR Art. 9). AntCV never infers or adds them. If you type one in, it appears only where you put it. Leave them out unless the job requires them.

Other people's data. When you name referees, ask them first. AntCV prints only their name and how they know you; contact details stay "on request".

Photo. Your browser checks which way the photo faces. Only when that check is unsure is the photo sent to a vision model, which answers "left", "right" or "centre". No face recognition, no emotion analysis.

6. Your rights

  • Access and portability (Art. 15, 20): Settings → Personal → Download my data (this browser) and Copy of my cloud data (everything stored on AntCV's servers, API key values masked).
  • Rectification (Art. 16): edit your profile at any time.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): Settings → Delete my account and all data removes your account and every stored row; you can save a copy first.
  • Restriction and objection (Art. 18, 21), including to the usage records: write to the contact in section 1.
  • Complaint: to Datatilsynet (Denmark, datatilsynet.dk) or the supervisory authority where you live.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted. Database rows are keyed by a hash of your e-mail address, not the address itself. API keys stay on the server and are never included in a data copy. Exports can be protected with a passphrase. A personal data breach is reported to the supervisory authority within 72 hours where the law requires it.
